The editor in the preview knows where a block's content comes from when it is not the block's own: entries of a collection and a global's values. Experimental, behind `NUXT_CMS_BUILDER=on`; without it the admin and the page are as before.

- **For an owner.** In a selected block, an entry it shows (a notice, a dish, a person of the team) shows its outline and its name when the pointer is over it (*Notices · Closed from 21 to 31 December*). A click selects it, with *Edit* and, under *…*, *Open in Notices*. A double click on one of its texts edits it with that field ready. *Edit* opens the entry's own form in a sheet beside the page, with its checks, languages, *Save* and publishing; what is typed there shows in the preview at once, and closing with unsaved changes asks first. The page's own changes and its selection stay as they were. A global's values drawn in a block (opening hours, the site's settings) work the same way. The panel of a selected block now says, under *Content from elsewhere*, which collections and globals it shows and lists the entries it shows now; a click on one edits it, *Add entry* adds one, *All entries* opens the list in a new tab. When a saved entry does not show in the section (a notice not valid today), a quiet line says so. A block's description in the panel is shown in full.
- **For a developer.** A block's component needs nothing for it. While an editor works on the page, the page keeps what it loads through the content API in the browser (`useFetch`, `$fetch` or `fetch` of `/api/content/…`), asks it with `?preview`, and loads once more what the server drew. The page sends the selected block's sources with `cms:builder:sources` and asks for an entry with `cms:builder:entry`; the admin sends the collections and globals with `entities` (and the entry being edited as `self`) on `cms:builder:on`. The entry's editor opens at its own address with `?embed=builder`, which the admin may frame only from its own origin and only with the setting on.
- **New text:** *Content from elsewhere*, *Add entry*, *All entries*, *Edit*, *Open in {name}* and a few more, in English, Czech and German.
