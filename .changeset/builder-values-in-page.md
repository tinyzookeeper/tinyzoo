The editor in the preview changes an entry's and a global's values where they stand in the page, and one save stores the page with them. Experimental, behind `NUXT_CMS_BUILDER=on`; without it the admin and the page are as before.

- **For an owner.** A plain text of an entry or a global that a block shows (a notice's title, a note in the opening hours, a dish's name) is typed right in the page, as the block's own texts are: a double click puts the cursor where it was, and so does a click in the text of a selected card. While it is typed, a quiet line says whose it is and that the change shows everywhere it appears. A value that is no plain text gets a small box over the page, at the element, with the field's own control: a number (a price drawn with its unit), a date, a choice, a rich text, a text with a form, and several values one element shows together (*7:00–8:00* opens *From* and *To* side by side). The box follows the page as it scrolls and closes on Escape, on Enter in a field of one line, on a press elsewhere, or when its element leaves the screen; *Whole entry* opens the entry's own form beside the page.
- **One save.** These changes wait in the page's editor with the page's own. The preview shows them at once, undo and redo hold them in the order they were made, and the editor's save stores the page and every changed entry and global, each through its own update route with the version it was loaded at. Beside the save button a line says what is saved when it is more than the page (*Page and 2 entries*); a click lists them, with the fields that changed, *Edit* and *Discard*. A save that is refused (a required field emptied, a newer version) says which entry and why, and keeps that change waiting; the others are saved. An entry's status is not changed. Leaving with such changes asks, as for the page's own.
- **The sheet beside the page.** An entry has one place where its changes are: when its own form opens beside the page, what was changed of it in the page goes into that form, and so does what is changed of it in the page while the form is open. A press elsewhere no longer asks to close the sheet; its own button and Escape do. A link in it to another admin page opens in a new tab.
- **Finding a value.** The page compares an element's text with the forms a value may be drawn in: a number plain, grouped and with a decimal comma, with a unit or a short word around it; a date and a time as the page's language writes them; a choice by an option's name; and several values of one entry, or of one row of a list, in a row. It is conservative: a value by its form counts only for what the block loads, a text several entries could have made is decided by the texts around it (the day beside the hours), a choice found alone needs more of its row in the block, and what stays unclear selects the card, where *Edit* works as before.
- **For a developer.** Additions to the `cms:builder:*` messages: `cms:builder:entry-text` (a typed text of an entry, with where it is inside the entry: a field, or an item of a list by its id), `cms:builder:value` and `cms:builder:anchor` (a box for values, and where its element is) to the admin; `cms:builder:pending` (what waits in the editor) and `cms:builder:box` to the page; on `cms:builder:on`, each collection's fields now say what a plain text takes, what a value is and what is inside a list and a group. The sheet's editor takes values with `cms:entry:set`.
- **New text:** *What will be saved*, *{page} and {others}*, *Changed: {fields}*, *Discard*, *Whole entry*, *Done* and a few more, in English, Czech and German.
