**Analytics: a friendlier Visitors page, with a Simple and a Detailed view.**

- The Analytics page is now called Visitors in English and German (Czech keeps Návštěvnost). It opens in a **Simple** view: one number and a few sentences in plain words on a cream moment ("1 284 people visited the website in the last 7 days. That's 179 more than the 7 days before. The busiest day was Tuesday…"), who is on the website right now, visitors as one warm line with the period before dashed, three numbers said the way a person would ("4 in 10 leave after one page"), and the lists as questions: What do they read? How do they find you? What do they use? Where are they from?
- The **Detailed** view has the usual terms (unique visitors, pageviews, bounce rate, average time), both lines, every list and tab (entry pages, websites, campaigns, UTM, browsers, systems, languages) and the comparison switch. Changes are shown as percentages without red or green: a quieter week is not an error.
- Each person's choice is remembered (cookie `cms_analytics_view`). Clicking a row still filters the whole page, in both views.
- The home page card says it in a sentence too, shows who is on the website now, and lists the most read pages by title.
- Visitors are drawn in a warm terracotta and pageviews in blue (checked for colour-blind readers and contrast in light and dark). The lists have a thin bar under each row, with room between rows, and their hover colour works again.
