# Editors' guide

How to write and look after your website's content in the admin: signing in, pages, images, translations, publishing, and what to do when something doesn't work.

## Who this guide is for

For the people who write and update what's on the website: news, pages, opening hours, photos, menus. You don't need to know how to code, or anything about how the website works inside. A web browser, an email address and a password are all you need.

You'll find the admin at your website's address with `/admin` on the end, for example `https://www.your-company.com/admin`. In it you change the website's **content** (text, images, menus). How the website looks, and which kinds of content it has, are set up by the developer who built it.

## How to read it

- Just want to sign in or write your first news item? Start with chapters one and three.
- Something isn't working? Jump straight to [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md). The most common problems are explained there in plain words.
- Text in **bold** is the name of a button or menu exactly as you'll see it on screen.
- Boxes marked **Tip** show an easier way to do something. Boxes marked **Watch out** warn you about things that are hard to undo.
- The parts of your website (such as **Pages**, **News** or **Site settings**) may have different names on your site. The steps are the same. This guide uses the names from the starter website.
